A gold theorem is covered only if the agent's theorem independently proves or asserts the gold's core claim, including all relevant preconditions and postconditions, and establishes a universal guarantee. Coverage requires a distinct property, not mere restatements, examples, or runtime checks. Theorems that redefine, recompute, or restate the same logic without proving new properties do not count. The agent must demonstrate the theorem's validity across all applicable cases, not just specific instances. Theorems that fail to address the gold's main claim or are weaker than the gold do not count as coverage.
