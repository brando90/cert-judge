## Scope and interpretation

The complaint inventory is 29 AI4Math rows, 14 MATH-AI rows, three TMLR rows, and nine rows from the now-populated DL4C ledger. All original IDs are retained below; complaint wording comes from those ledgers.

**Answered** means the revised source supplies the requested explanation, result, or structural change. **Disclosed** means the shortcoming remains and is explicitly stated. **Superseded** means the criticized artifact or venue-specific decision no longer applies.

Quoted sentences reproduce current source, including LaTeX; the opening caption command is omitted for caption quotes. For removed artifacts and mechanical checks, a manuscript sentence cannot prove absence: the assessment explicitly separates representative prose from structural evidence.
