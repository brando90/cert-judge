# Certification-judge progress for the Nikhil meeting

**TLDR:** We have a reproducible negative result for the current Proxy Trust Index and a new diagnostic explaining why pooled human agreement needs closer inspection. A trustworthy proxy that generalizes across agents is still an open research objective, not an established result.

Updated: 2026-09-13 12:11 PDT. Version3 passed independent acceptance review; both reported minor defects are fixed and 90 tests pass. The version3 report is published and verified, and pull request43 is merged at `63e6a84`; the local checkout is on main and synchronized. Version2 is also on main.

## What is verified

- Experiment29 completed its frozen eight-judge scoring panel: five primary configurations and three controls; there are only two primary model families.
- Experiment30 version2 tested the intended geometric mean of human agreement and repeat stability: primary-panel Spearman is −0.354 for the original proxy and −0.600 for the 0.001-floor sensitivity, with both problem-bootstrap intervals crossing zero.
- New version3 compares answers to the same problem. String-control ordering accuracy is 47.7%; Sonnet checklist is 57.6%; their difference interval crosses zero. There are 22 informative problems and 52 human-unequal candidate pairs. This is a different estimand from pooled rank correlation, not proof that the labels or either judge are wrong.
- A six-problem, 18-candidate development inventory is now reproducible and independent of outcomes; its original request identities and all 480 cached repeat scores are preserved, but it lacks bug and missing-specification probes and cannot yet validate the complete proxy.

## What this changes

The string control's pooled agreement does not establish good ordering within a problem. The inconclusive diagnostic leaves task composition and the intended meaning of the human criterion unresolved; it does not identify either as the cause. Zero-flooring alone has already failed to establish proxy validity. Additional prompts from the same models do not establish independent-family generalization.

## Decision for the discussion

[p9/10] Agree what the criterion should measure for candidate answers to the same problem, because that determines whether a targeted blinded content audit or a broader judge panel is the next useful test.

[p8/10] Freeze a genuinely new-family development/validation/test design and exact workload once that target is clear, because the exposed 72 candidates cannot be reused as an untouched final test.
