Panel diagnostics compare each family/persona with both the primary target and the target excluding that family. Raw panel ratings remain private.

Intervals condition on the existing simulated panel and frozen procedures; they omit new human-rater and new model-family uncertainty. Primary/common-target correlations among cached procedures may be optimistic because prompts and the simulated panel are related.
