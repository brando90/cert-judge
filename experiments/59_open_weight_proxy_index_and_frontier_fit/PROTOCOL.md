# Experiment 59 protocol: proxy indices on open-weight judges, and fitting open-weight judges to frontier judges

**TLDR:** Two questions, both answered from cached frontier-judge outputs plus new open-weight judge runs on free GPUs ($0, no paid API). (1) Does a label-free index (experiment 39's frozen `C_ms_gold`, plus two new "agreement with the frontier consensus" indices) rank open-weight judge configurations by their agreement with the VeriBench expert ratings? (2) Can open-weight judge scores be combined (selection, averaging, ridge or non-negative stacking) and fit to the frontier consensus, using no human labels in the fit, so that the result tracks the human ratings on held-out tasks? Everything below is frozen in the commit that adds this file; no new judge output had been produced or read when it was committed. Any later change is a dated addendum at the end, never an edit.

**Status:** FROZEN at the commit that adds this file. Judge calls made before it: 0 (stage 1 launches after this commit).

## 3. Part 1: label-free indices of an open-weight judge (the transfer question)

Per open-weight unit u compute, with no human label:

- `C_ms_gold` (frozen, experiment 39's `components()['ms_gold']`): the primary index.
- `F1_probe`: Spearman between u's cell scores and `T`, over the non-criterion cells (kinds `ident`, `blank`, `ms_gold`, `bm_gold` and the other probe kinds), needing at least 80% of those cells scored.
- `F2_item`: Spearman between u's item medians and `T`'s item medians over the 75 criterion items (no human label is read; the items are the same ones the criterion uses, disclosed), needing 90% of items scored.
- `F12` = geometric mean of `max(F1_probe,0)` and `max(F2_item,0)`.

**Primary (gate, unchanged from experiments 39 and 58):** Spearman across the open-weight units between `C_ms_gold` and the criterion, one-sided permutation p, 10,000 draws, seed 39 (`analysis_lib.association`), PASS iff rho >= 0.45 and p < 0.05; reported together with the model-clustered permutation and model-cluster bootstrap (seed 59). Plain and clustered results are stated separately; a plain PASS with a clustered FAIL is reported as exactly that.
**Secondary (same statistic, same gate, no multiplicity adjustment, labelled exploratory):** `F1_probe`, `F2_item`, `F12`. The two probe-based indices (`C_ms_gold`, `F1_probe`) see only gold-file probes; `F2_item` sees the rated candidates but not the ratings. The paper's frozen `PTI_v0` and `TI_var` are reported on the same units, no gate. Also reported: the same associations over (i) the 56 hosted units of experiment 39, (ii) hosted plus new units, (iii) each prompt family, (iv) within each model. The criterion's split-half reliability is the attenuation bound, as in experiment 39.

## 4. Part 2: fitting open-weight judges to the frontier consensus (the fit question)

Fit data use no human label: the target is `T`. Human ratings are used only for evaluation (and for the diagnostic learner L4 below).

Rows: the cells of experiment 39 (probe cells and criterion-item cells) with features = scores of the open-weight units, the columns standardized with train statistics, missing scores imputed by the column's train median. Fit rows come from train-split tasks only; the validation split chooses hyperparameters; the test split's 10 tasks (30 human-rated items, and their probe cells) are never read until the learners are frozen and committed.

Learners (all frozen here):
- **L0 best-single:** the open unit with the largest `F1_probe` on train+val tasks' probe cells.
- **L1 top-k mean:** the mean of the rank-normalized scores of the k = 5 open units with the largest `F1_probe` (train+val tasks).
- **L2 ridge stack:** ridge regression of `T` on all open-unit columns; alpha chosen from {0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000} by validation-task mean squared error, then refit on train+val rows.
- **L3 NNLS stack:** non-negative least squares of `T` on the open-unit columns, refit on train+val rows (interpretable weights; reported).
- **L4 human-fit (diagnostic, uses labels):** ridge on the 30 train human-rated items' expert mean, alpha by the 15 val items. Shows what the human labels add.

Evaluation (once, test split): for each learner, the Spearman correlation (also Pearson, Kendall, ICC(C,1), ICC(A,1)) between the learner's score on each test item (median over the 3 repeats of cells) and the expert mean over the 30 test items, with task-clustered bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000 draws, seed 59), next to the same numbers for `R_front`, `R_validated`, `R_local_default`, and an oracle row, the best single open unit by test criterion (optimistic, labelled oracle). Paired task-bootstrap differences: L1/L2/L3 minus L0, and each minus `R_front`. Secondary, descriptive: 5-fold task-grouped cross-validation over all 25 tasks (fixed fold assignment, seed 59) of L0-L3 against the 75 items' expert mean.

## 5. Decision rules (frozen)

- **Part 1 verdict:** PASS iff the primary gate passes (plain) and the model-clustered permutation p < 0.10; otherwise FAIL if plain fails, INCONCLUSIVE if only the clustered test fails. Secondary indices are reported as holds/does not hold on the same gate and are never promoted to the verdict.
- **Part 2 verdict:** FIT-PASS iff the best of L1/L2/L3 (chosen by validation, not test) has test Spearman >= 0.45 AND its 95% interval's lower end is above 0 AND its point estimate exceeds L0's. FIT-NO-GAIN if some learner clears 0.45 but none beats L0. FIT-FAIL otherwise. With 30 items from 10 tasks the interval half-width is about 0.3, so INCONCLUSIVE-by-width is stated whenever the interval contains both 0 and `R_front`'s point estimate. Nothing claims an open-weight fit "matches" a frontier judge from this experiment.
- **What would change the plan (written now):** if more than 20% of a model's cells are `incomplete` or `parse_error`, its units are flagged, and both analyses are shown with and without them. If fewer than 8 open units have both numbers, no association is computed. A model that cannot load is `unmeasured_infra` for all its units, never dropped silently.
