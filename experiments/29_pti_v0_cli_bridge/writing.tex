\paragraph{PTI-v0 execution.}
We evaluated the frozen four-property proxy on five primary judge--prompt
configurations and three controls.  The run used locally authenticated
subscription CLIs, completed all 4,728 unique score cells with three repeats
per cell, and incurred zero provider-API spend.  The exact v4 model panel was
not run: this versioned v5 protocol used \texttt{gpt-5.6-terra} and requested
\texttt{claude-sonnet-5}.  Claude's retained telemetry additionally reports a
small \texttt{claude-haiku-4-5} helper call on every Claude invocation, so we do
not describe those runs as Sonnet-only.  Unrelated cases shared a batch context,
but artifact-disjoint packing prevented levels or transformation partners of a
typed artifact from appearing together.

\paragraph{Property screen.}
All four property estimates were computable at complete coverage.  Under the
pre-registered exact-zero-first geometric mean, four of five primary
configurations obtained \(\mathrm{PTI}_{\mathrm{v0}}=0\); each zero was traced
to at least one observed zero artifact factor rather than to missing data.  The
Sonnet--P8 configuration obtained
\(\mathrm{PTI}_{\mathrm{v0}}=0.8114\), with a 95\% artifact-cluster bootstrap
interval of \([0.7820,0.8391]\).  Passing remains a behavioral screen result,
not proof of trustworthiness.
