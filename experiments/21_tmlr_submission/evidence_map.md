## SUPERSEDING UPDATE (2026-07-21, CORRECTED same day by stage-1 QA) — label-protocol drift confounds exp43; score files are NOT corrupt

The dir-22 audit (`experiments/22_pooled_heldout_and_noise_ceiling/REPORT.md`) first claimed `results_exp16_final_robust_r9_median/detail_rows.csv` cannot reproduce the published `judge_rows.csv` correlations. **Stage-1 QA refuted that claim by independent recomputation**: `detail_rows.csv` reproduces every published calibration correlation to machine precision (all 8 judges × pass1/pass2/avg, |Δ| < 1e-12) when joined with the label files exp16 actually used (`…/04_vb/data/ethan_human_scores_*.tsv`); the audit's near-zero recomputations came from joining against `experiments/human_labels/expert_harsh_set_data.tsv` + `lenient_set_data.tsv` — a *different annotation campaign*. The verified real finding: **labeling-protocol drift** — calibration labels (old-protocol campaign, also the basis of TI_var development) correlate only ρ≈0.13 (n=63) with the revised-protocol five-rater pool; the same annotator agrees with the pool at ρ=0.90 under the revised protocol but with their own old-protocol labels at only ρ≈0.18. Consequences for Priority 2 above: **exp43's per-family numbers are valid computations of a cross-protocol comparison and cannot answer the TI_var transfer question (OPEN, confounded — not corruption-voided)**; the clean judge-level fact stands (judges track old-protocol labels at up to 0.47 but the revised-protocol pool at ≈0; median −0.24 over 14 real configs, best +0.42 LeanStral P1); **exp42 is one-protocol (pool-only) and NOT confounded — its null/negative zero-leakage transfer numbers stand as valid**; exp41/exp38 keep their oracle-only labels; `detail_rows.csv` needs no quarantine. Verified label-side results: pool ceiling ≈0.87 (pairwise 0.42, K_eff 4.4), LOO mean 0.57, revised-protocol strict-pass set vs pool ρ=0.96 repeat-averaged (0.899 last-wins), annotator relabel vs pool ρ=0.90. Decisive next step: **one labeling protocol end-to-end** (re-anchor calibration to the revised-protocol construct, then rerun the frozen test within-protocol); judge re-scoring is optional, not a validity gate. Label-lineage hazard to respect forever: `experiments/human_labels/` (revised protocol) vs `…/04_vb/data/ethan_human_scores_*` (old protocol) are near-uncorrelated label sets — never join them interchangeably.
