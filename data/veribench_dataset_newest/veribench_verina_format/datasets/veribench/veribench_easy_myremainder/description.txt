-----Description-----
Compute the remainder when dividing a natural number `a` by a natural number divisor `b` (intended for positive `b`).

-----Input-----
- a: a natural number (the dividend)
- b: a natural number (the divisor; should be non-zero)

-----Output-----
A natural number equal to `a % b`, i.e., the remainder of dividing `a` by `b`.

-----Note-----
The file defines a precondition `Pre a b := b > 0`; inputs with `b = 0` violate this intended usage.
