-----Description-----
Compute the parity (0 for even, 1 for odd) of the sum of all natural numbers in a list.

-----Input-----
- xs: A list of natural numbers (List Nat)

-----Output-----
A natural number 0 or 1 equal to (sum of all elements in xs) mod 2.

-----Note-----
The implementation sums the list and then takes the result modulo 2. The precondition allows any list.
