-----Description-----
Compute the sample covariance between two lists of rational numbers.

-----Input-----
- x: a list of rational numbers
- y: a list of rational numbers

-----Output-----
A rational number equal to the sample covariance of x and y (using denominator n-1), or 0 if the lists have different lengths or have length < 2.

-----Note-----
The mathematical precondition for intended use is that x and y have the same length and that length is at least 2.
