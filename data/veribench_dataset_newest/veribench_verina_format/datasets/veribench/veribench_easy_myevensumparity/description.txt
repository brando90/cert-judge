-----Description-----
Compute the parity (0 for even, 1 for odd) of the sum of all natural numbers in a list.

-----Input-----
- xs: A list of natural numbers

-----Output-----
- A natural number equal to (sum of xs) mod 2, i.e., 0 or 1

-----Note-----
The intended precondition in the file states that all elements of the list are even; under that precondition the result is always 0.
