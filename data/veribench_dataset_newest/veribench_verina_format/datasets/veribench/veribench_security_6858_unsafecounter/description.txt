-----Description-----
Increment a counter’s value by 1, modeling a non-atomic (unsafe) increment that can lead to race conditions in concurrent settings.

-----Input-----
- counter: A Counter record containing an integer field `value`

-----Output-----
A new Counter whose `value` is the input counter’s value plus 1.

-----Note-----
This models an unsafe, non-atomic increment; in concurrent execution, multiple increments may lead to lost updates (though this behavior is not simulated by the pure function itself).
