-----Description-----
Compute four times the absolute difference between two natural numbers `a` and `b`, then add 3.

-----Input-----
- a: a natural number
- b: a natural number

-----Output-----
- Returns `4 * |a - b| + 3` as a natural number.

-----Note-----
- The absolute difference is computed as `a - b` if `a ≥ b`, otherwise `b - a`.
- The result is always at least 3, and the function is commutative in its inputs.
