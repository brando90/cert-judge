\section{Conclusion}
We proposed an amortized protocol for validating LLM judges of formal specifications: collect human labels once, then screen each new judge with falsifiable properties whose correct direction is known by construction.
On a 72-pair validation set, a variance-aware trust index built from bug monotonicity and score stability is positively associated with judge-level human agreement in all three judge families, and it flags a lexical judge that human agreement alone would keep.
Specification monotonicity carries the strongest signal.
The key next step is a locked held-out test with independent annotators under one frozen rubric.
