\subsection*{Bottom line}
Across live VeriBench ratings (\texttt{native\_main}, $N=100$ files, $250$ non-\texttt{A1} ratings, $33/33$ public Sheets unchanged vs commit \texttt{460466d}, \texttt{CONST\_loto RPS} $= 0.0698$, \texttt{CE} $= 0.8716$, \texttt{TVD} $= 0.3636$), \texttt{E39+E42} ($60$ units), \texttt{E60} ($62$ open-weight harvests), \texttt{E58} ($117$ units), and \texttt{E63} ($6$ arms), evaluating proxies against proper-scoring targets via decision-theoretic regret, utility-weighted pairwise accuracy (\texttt{UWA}), and held-out \texttt{Beat-CONST Meta-Brier} eliminates double-correlation bias and control hijacking. On held-out \texttt{test\_llm} ($n=18$), label-free \texttt{P\_e42\_probe\_rps} achieves \texttt{UWA\_RPS} $= 0.8614$ ($95\%$ CI $[0.650, 0.974]$) on \texttt{ce\_cal\_all5\_rps} and $0.9173$ ($[0.742, 0.981]$) on \texttt{raw\_all5\_rps}, and \texttt{P\_e42\_strict\_brier} achieves held-out \texttt{Beat-CONST Meta-Brier} $= 0.0549$ (vs base rate $0.256$, $\mathrm{AUROC} = 0.883$) and $0.0453$ (vs $0.251$) with controls added. On \texttt{E60 seed1} ($n=36$), \texttt{P\_e39\_fullcov\_brier} beats \texttt{C\_ms\_gold} on both \texttt{ce\_cal\_all5\_rps} (\texttt{UWA} $0.7686$ vs $0.6755$, Spearman $0.5271$ $[0.258, 0.715]$ $p=0.0016$ vs $0.3073$ $p=0.0688$) and \texttt{raw\_all5\_rps} (\texttt{UWA} $0.8514$ vs $0.6164$).

\subsection*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{ctl|editdist} achieves \texttt{P\_e42\_fullcov\_brier} $= 0.8195$ (above the pre-registered $0.78$ bound) because edit similarity rejects every modified defective file; Top-1 LLM selection ($0.9553$--$0.9844$) and formatting-aware \texttt{P\_e42\_disc} separate real judges.
  \item In the zero-leakage head-to-head (\texttt{38} \texttt{train+val} LLMs), \texttt{Arm B} (\texttt{Meta-Proper-RPS}) picked \texttt{P\_dsti\_gated}, which missed \texttt{qwen36|H2}/\texttt{R4} and lost out-of-sample on \texttt{test\_llm} to \texttt{Arm A}'s \texttt{P\_e42\_strict\_brier} (\texttt{Top-1} $0.0379$ vs $0.0146$, \texttt{Sub-10} $0.0237$ vs $0.0138$). With $\alpha = 0.5$, finite-$m$ ceilings are $0.9844$ (\texttt{P\_e42\_fullcov\_brier}) and $0.9609$ (\texttt{P\_e39\_fullcov\_brier}).
  \item Live \texttt{native\_main} ($250$ non-\texttt{A1} ratings) has a $74.0\%$ score-5 ceiling ($185/250$); Google Drive and Gmail MCP connectors returned \texttt{Unauthorized: rejected by transport} (companion Doc staged locally).
  \item Fresh paid judge probes remain paused under the \texttt{10-15-2026} Valkyrie spending pause; \texttt{claude-opus-4-7|P8\_STRICT\_B} maps to same-family \texttt{opus55x|P4}.
\end{itemize}
